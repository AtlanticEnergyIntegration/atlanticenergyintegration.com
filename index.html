IEC IEEE

Infrastructure integration in cross-border environments requires navigating overlapping IEC and IEEE standards — from distributed generation interconnection (IEEE 1547) and grid protection coordination, to IEC 61850 control system communication and IEC 61968 utility integration frameworks. Aligning these standards across jurisdictions and stakeholder requirements is a core technical coordination competency at AEI.

About Atlantic Energy Integration

A Boutique Infrastructure Advisory and Integration Firm

Atlantic Energy Integration operates as an independent infrastructure advisor and systems integrator. The company's focus is distributed energy infrastructure, CHP systems, microgrid-capable environments, and owner-side project coordination.

AEI maintains operational presence across the United States, Ukraine, and the European Union — enabling coordination across jurisdictions with differing infrastructure standards and procurement frameworks.

The firm's approach prioritizes technical specificity, owner-side alignment, and operational realism over generalized advisory services or vendor-oriented recommendations.
