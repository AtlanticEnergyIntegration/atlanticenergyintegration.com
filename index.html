About

The U.S.–International
Energy Bridge

Atlantic Energy Integration LLC is a Boston-based energy infrastructure company specializing in the integration of U.S.-manufactured distributed energy technologies into international markets — with a primary focus on Ukraine and Eastern Europe.

We serve as the operational bridge between American technology providers and international project stakeholders — managing the full cycle from technology selection and commercial structuring through to in-country deployment coordination.

Our work spans distributed generation, critical infrastructure resilience, and industrial energy systems — combining deep technical expertise with established commercial relationships across the U.S. and international markets.

Technical Integration

End-to-end engineering coordination for distributed generation and microgrid projects — from system design and equipment specification through commissioning and operational handover.

International Project Development

Structured project development for cross-border energy infrastructure initiatives — navigating regulatory, procurement, and commercial requirements across U.S. and international jurisdictions.

In-Country Execution Capability

Established partnerships with accredited engineering organizations in target markets, providing full local execution capacity — design, testing, certification, and construction management.

Cybersecurity Integration

ICS/OT security expertise embedded in all project designs, aligned with CISA Cyber-Informed Engineering (CIE) framework and U.S. critical infrastructure standards.

Services

What We Deliver

End-to-end integration of U.S. energy technologies into international infrastructure environments — from feasibility to commissioning.

01
Integrated Systems Deployment

Containerized distributed power units, modular generation systems, and hybrid microgrid integration — sourced from leading U.S. manufacturers and adapted to local technical and regulatory requirements.

02
Strategic Advisory & Feasibility

Technical and economic feasibility studies, infrastructure resilience master plans, and U.S.–International Energy Integration Program development for reconstruction projects.

03
Project Management & Integration

End-to-end coordination between U.S. equipment suppliers, in-country EPC partners, and project stakeholders — including regulatory compliance and certification support.

04
Manufacturer Representation

Representing U.S. manufacturers in Ukrainian and Eastern European markets — handling technical adaptation, certification of imported equipment, and local market entry.

05
ICS/OT Cybersecurity Integration

Cyber-informed engineering assessments for energy infrastructure, aligned with CISA CIE framework. Securing industrial control systems in distributed generation deployments.

06
Supply Chain Optimization

Connecting international project developers with verified U.S. and European equipment suppliers. Priority sourcing of industrial automation components through established partnerships.
