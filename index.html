About

The U.S.–International
Energy Bridge

Atlantic Energy Integration LLC is a Boston-based energy infrastructure integrator acting as the strategic link between U.S. manufacturers of distributed energy technology and international markets — primarily Ukraine and Eastern Europe — undergoing significant infrastructure modernization.

Our founder brings nearly a decade of operational experience within Ukraine's national gas transmission system (Ukrtransgaz / GTSOU), combined with U.S.-based cybersecurity and infrastructure expertise developed through a fellowship with the City of Boston.

We operate at the intersection of technical integration, regulatory compliance, and international project management — offering a service model that no pure engineering firm or general consultancy can replicate.

Memberships: IEEE  ·  AEE  ·  CS²AI

Services

What We Deliver

End-to-end integration of U.S. energy technologies into international infrastructure environments — from feasibility to commissioning.

01
Integrated Systems Deployment

Containerized CHP modules, modular distributed power units, and hybrid microgrid integration. Sourced from U.S. manufacturers including Caterpillar, Cummins, and Eaton.

02
Strategic Advisory & Feasibility

Technical and economic feasibility studies, infrastructure resilience master plans, and U.S.–International Energy Integration Program development for reconstruction projects.

03
Project Management & Integration

End-to-end coordination between U.S. equipment suppliers, in-country EPC partners, and project stakeholders — including regulatory compliance and certification support.

04
Manufacturer Representation

Representing U.S. manufacturers in Ukrainian and Eastern European markets — handling technical adaptation, certification of imported equipment, and local market entry.

05
ICS/OT Cybersecurity Integration

Cyber-informed engineering assessments for energy infrastructure, aligned with CISA CIE framework. Securing industrial control systems in distributed generation deployments.

06
Supply Chain Optimization

Connecting international project developers with verified U.S. and European equipment suppliers. Priority sourcing of industrial automation components through established partnerships.
